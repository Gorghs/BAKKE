BAKKE demo runbook

Open http://localhost:3000

The app is up. The worker is idle and the database holds only the five seeded
cases. You create the case yourself from the form on the front page.

Step 1: create the case

On "/" fill the create form.

  Name:        Missing tools
  Description: Hand tools were taken from a locked store on the night of
               5 March. The store was locked at 18:05 and found open at
               07:50 the next morning.

Submit. You land on the case page.

Step 2: add five evidence items

Use the evidence form on the case page. For each item choose the type, give it
a title, and paste the text. Add them one at a time.

Item 1, type WITNESS_STATEMENT

Title: Depot supervisor statement, 6 March
Text:
I am Ravi Menon, depot supervisor. At 18:05 on 5 March I locked the tool store
myself and handed the only key to the night guard, Suresh. The store has one
door on the north side and no window. When I opened the yard at 07:50 on
6 March the store door was standing open and the padlock was gone. Suresh was
on duty all night and says he checked the yard twice, once around midnight and
once at about 04:00.

Item 2, type DIGITAL_RECORD

Title: Gate log extract, 5-6 March
Text:
Gate log, 5 March to 6 March. The last vehicle left the yard at 19:12 on
5 March, registered to Menon Transport. No vehicle entered the yard after
19:12 and before 07:50 on 6 March. The north yard camera feed stopped
recording at 21:40 on 5 March. No camera fault was logged and the recorder
stayed powered. The gate was closed and latched at 19:12.

Item 3, type PHYSICAL_EVIDENCE

Title: Store inventory note, 6 March
Text:
Tool store inventory, checked 6 March. The store holds hand tools only:
spanners, screwdrivers, two hacksaws and a torque wrench. No cutting
equipment, no chemicals and no fuel are kept in the store. The padlock is a
standard brass padlock and two keys were cut for it. One key was on the
supervisor's ring, the other was handed to the night guard. The padlock was
not recovered.

Item 4, type LOCATION_RECORD

Title: Yard layout record
Text:
North yard layout. The tool store door faces the north yard. The yard has one
gate on the east side. The north wall is solid and windowless. The camera on
the north wall covers the store door and the gate together. The store is
roughly 12 metres from the gate. The yard has no lighting on the north side.

Item 5, type WITNESS_STATEMENT

Title: Night guard statement, 6 March
Text:
I am Suresh, night guard. I came on duty at 19:00 on 5 March and stayed until
07:00 on 6 March. At about midnight I walked the yard and the store door was
shut. At about 04:00 I walked the yard again and the door was shut. I did not
go into the store at any point. I kept the store key on my belt. At 07:50 I
opened the gate for Mr Menon and we found the door open together.

Step 3: analyse

Click Analyse on the case page. The job goes to the worker.

Watch the counters. The worker runs one job at a time and the run takes a few
minutes. Do not start a second analysis while one is running.

When it finishes, the dashboard should show a non-zero timeline count and at
least one surviving scenario.

Step 4: walk the tabs

Evidence    the five items
Facts       extracted facts, each one source-linked
Timeline    events with times. This is the tab that used to come back empty
Constraints the checks that ran
Scenarios   survivors and rejections, with reasons
Audit       the run trail
Video       the visual-only step

For the video step, case 5241341d (clinic) already has a READY video, so you
can play that tab without generating anything new. A fresh generation needs a
current Gemini web cookie, see below.

If something goes wrong

The analysis needs the Groq route. If a job fails with a connection error from
inside the container, the container lost its network namespace. Fix it with

    docker compose down && docker compose up -d

The free route caps output at 1000 tokens per minute. The extraction writes at
most 12 facts and puts the timeline first for that reason. If a run returns 0
timeline events, check that the container runs the committed code.

Video generation needs a current cookie in ~/Projects/gemini-web-to-api/.env
(GEMINI_COOKIES), then

    cd ~/Projects/gemini-web-to-api && docker compose restart app

The cookie lasts about 75 to 90 minutes.
